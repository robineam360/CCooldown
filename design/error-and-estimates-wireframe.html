CCRM-27 · CCRM-30 — wireframe for approval

Typed errors, honest estimates

Two changes to how the app talks about what it doesn't know. CCRM-27 (Error Taxonomy): the bare Status: HTTP 529 line becomes a tinted notice row whose copy names the fix, driven by a typed error kind stored beside the status string. CCRM-30 (Estimate Honesty): every inferred number carries a subtle marker and a tap-to-reveal provenance line, so an inference never wears a measurement's confidence. The retained last-good snapshot keeps rendering beside the error in both — that behaviour is load-bearing and untouched.

2 · CCRM-30 — mark inferred numbers as inferred

Mechanism: inferred figures get a dotted underline and the line becomes tappable; a tap reveals a one-line provenance note under it (tap again to hide). Subtle at rest, explicit on demand — no info icons spent on every line, nothing for a screenshot to misread as data. Widgets and the notification get nothing (too tight, per the entry).
