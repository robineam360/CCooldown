1 · CCRM-34 (Diagnostics Log) — Settings → Diagnostics

`PingLog` generalised into diag/AppLog.kt: levelled (Error/Warn/Info/Debug), categorised (poll · alerts · auth · ping), one lock across the background writers, same pullable external-files location (app-log.txt). Minimum level is a user-facing pref, default Info. Logged: poll outcomes (success at Debug), alert posts and permission blocks, token-renewal outcomes as status codes only, every ping event as before. Hard rule enforced in the object's contract: never tokens, headers, or the code_verifier. The card moved out of the debug unlock into its own visible Diagnostics section — "share your log" can't hide behind a 7-tap ritual.

Settings → Diagnostics

App log
What the app does in the background — polls, alerts, sign-in renewals, ping alarms. Share it when reporting a problem; it never contains tokens.

Dynamic shortcuts (labels follow renamed profiles, republished on launch and on rename): the profile entries reuse the "profile" extra every other entry point sends; Refresh now opens the app with a refresh extra that triggers a manual poll of every account.
