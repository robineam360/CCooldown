CCRM-44 · wireframe for approval

One surface for everything

When the always-on notification is on and showing a profile, that profile posts no other notification, ever — silent by choice. Every alert becomes a strip inside the pinned panel, readable on demand. When the pinned notification is off (or not showing that profile), nothing changes from today: alerts post standalone. Extends the CCBG-12 (Status Icon Swap) conditions mechanism from two conditions to the full alert set.

5 · Settings copy change

The always-on toggle's subtitle gains one sentence so silence is a stated contract, not a surprise:

Always-on usage notification
A silent, ongoing notification with a status-bar icon that fills as you use your 5-hour window. While it's on, all alerts fold into this panel instead of posting separately — no sounds, no pop-ups.

6 · Consequences accepted with this design

· No alert ever makes a sound or heads-up while the pinned notification shows that profile — missing a "will run out" until the shade is opened is the accepted trade.
· The app can no longer produce two simultaneous notifications per profile while pinned is on, so the CCBG-12 group summary becomes a pinned-off-only safety net — it stays in the code.
· alertLifetime's meaning shifts from "when the standalone alert expires" to "when the strip clears"; same setting, same values, same Auto.
