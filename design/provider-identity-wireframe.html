Rev B redraws the three things that changed on review, so they can be confirmed before build: provider logos where rev A had a dot, a per-account accent override where rev A only had the default, and hidden where rev A had a placeholder card for a window the account does not have. Fixed: applicationId com.robin.claudeusage, the namespace and the CCooldown repo do not change — a new id is a new app and every install, widget and year of history is lost. Only the label, icon and docs move.

Decision 1 — three-level accent. An account's bars, ring and theme use: its own Accent colour… override if set → else the global theme choice if it is not "Per provider" → else the provider's colour. The global picker is what exists today, plus a first swatch Per provider; existing installs that never touched the picker land on it and look pixel-identical. The per-account override is new (section 4).

Decision 7 (taken under recommendation, confirm here). The Blue family's pace-line partner is spring green, a near neighbour of the ChatGPT accent. On any face that draws more than one account (Mini-Rings, the pinned panel's folded strips) pace ticks are neutral ink — what CCRM-51 (Rails Gauge) already does on the status bar. Single-account faces keep the theme's cool partner.

3 · Icon decision 3 · hourglass, three sands, all three colours now

Rev A's sands were thin. Rev B fills the lower bulb higher and gives each colour a band that survives 48 dp: blue at the base, green, then the orange that is pouring — the top sand and the falling grain turn orange too, so the icon reads "the Claude window is running" without a percentage. The ground moves from burnt orange to slate so all three colours sit on neutral. Constraints from CCRM-45 (Tracker Icon) hold: no readable percentage at 16 px, no third party's mark in the launcher icon.
