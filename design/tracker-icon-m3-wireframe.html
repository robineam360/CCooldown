CCRM-45 · wireframe, round 3 — Material 3 treatment

Tracker icon, the Material round

Round-2 feedback: too simple, too flat. This round is proper Material 3 — a tonal palette built from Claude orange, a radial-gradient background instead of a flat tile, M3-expressive container shapes (the scalloped "cookie") behind the glyph, and layered, multi-tone glyphs instead of plain white strokes. The CCBG-12 (Status Icon Swap) constraint still governs: nothing may read as a percentage at 16 px.

The palette — Claude orange, tonalised

An M3-style tonal ladder seeded from the app's Claude Orange, plus the cream the brand pairs with. The background gradient runs 60 → 30; glyphs use 95/90/80 over it; the espresso 20 anchors detail. (Themed/monochrome icons keep following the system's dynamic tones as they do today.)

The options, re-dressed

A · Signal steps on a cookie

The three ascending bars, now tonal (90 → 95 gradient fills with an espresso key bar) on an M3 scalloped-cookie container in tone 40, over the gradient ground.

6:32 · mono flattens to the trio — unchanged

Depth trick: the tallest bar goes espresso-dark, not light — the eye reads highest-usage as heaviest. Cookie spins 15° on the themed layer for the M3 "shape morph" feel.

B · Hourglass, tonal sand

Cream glass on a tone-40 circle container, sand in a 90→80 gradient mid-pour — a warm, dimensional object instead of a stroke drawing.

6:32 · filled silhouette at 16 px, no strokes

Depth trick: filled sand shapes + a falling grain line; the mono/status version switches to one solid silhouette so nothing thins out.

C · Tonal dial

The tick dial becomes an M3 gauge object: three arc segments in tones 95 / 80 / 20 with round caps and gaps (complete and symmetric — an instrument, not a reading), pivot dot in cream.

6:32 · four equal segments, tone lost, shape kept

Note: the needle from round 2 is gone — tones carry the "instrument" idea instead, which also deletes that option's residual gauge-ness. This makes C and D siblings; C is the tonal one.

D · Four windows, four tones

The four-window ring re-dressed: each 5-hour-window segment gets its own tone (95 → 90 → 80 → espresso 20), turning the identity into a tonal sweep around the day. Sits straight on the gradient, no container.

6:32 · segments hold; tones flatten to one

Watch for: the darkest (espresso) segment nearly merges with a dark gradient corner at 48 px — if D wins, the espresso segment gets a 1 dp cream keyline. Caveat: four *different* tones can whisper "pie chart"; the equal segment lengths are what keep it identity, not data.

Implementation notes

· Adaptive icon: background layer = the radial gradient (60 → 40 → 30) as a vector drawable; foreground layer = the chosen glyph cluster. Launchers mask their own shape over both, so nothing here depends on the squircle.
· The themed (Material You) icon stays a single-tone monochrome layer — the system recolours it — which is why every option keeps a solid-shape mono variant.
· Status bar at 16 px is monochrome by platform rule; the "status strip" rows above show exactly that flattening.
· Still true for all four: no state, no level, no percentage — CCBG-12's bar.
