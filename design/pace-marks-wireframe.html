Pace Marks on the Widget Rings

Every circular gauge gains a mark at the even-pace point — where the fill tip would sit if usage were spread evenly across the window. Read it like a watch bezel: fill tip past the mark (clockwise) means above pace, short of it means headroom. The mark's position is Fmt.elapsedPercent — the same number the pace sentence and chart use — so the ring and the prose can never disagree. All faces below show a 5-hour window that is 55% elapsed.

Decisions encoded above

  • Mark position is Fmt.elapsedPercent, computed per window at render time — the identical input the pace verdict and large-widget chart already use, so the three surfaces can't drift apart.
  • Neutral colour, drawn topmost. Black/white at ~50–60% opacity, above the fill, extending ~4 pt past both stroke edges — legible whether it lands on bare track, accent fill, or a warning hue. In vibrant/tinted rendering it becomes Color.primary at the same opacity, on top of the existing opacity-ladder fill.
  • No dead-zone rendering. The ±3 pt paceDeadZone stays in the verdict logic (sentence, colours); drawing it as an arc segment would make the tick a gauge of its own. The tick is purely positional.
  • When the tick doesn't draw: no data (bare track), no reset timestamp (elapsedPercent nil), and every message-only state. Same rule as the fill: never fake a value.
  • Geometry lives in RingGauge — one new optional paceMark: Double? parameter serves both the small hero ring and the medium mini-rings; the large widget keeps its chart and gains nothing here.
  • Over-pace segment — the chart-wash rule translated to the stroke: drawn only when the verdict is .above (fill > elapsed + paceDeadZone, so it can never contradict the pace sentence and never flickers on a hairline overshoot), from the tick to the fill tip, in the window's role colour blended 50% toward the red role (A1) — the severity ladder stays the base coat, the wash rides on top, and a ring never wears full ≥100% red before it's earned. Under the tick in z-order; the tick stays topmost. In vibrant/tinted rendering the segment falls back to the next rung of the existing opacity ladder.
Decision (user, 2026-08-13): Option A tick + A2 full-red segment. Among the marks, the dot (B) is ambiguous — the fill's round cap already ends in a dot shape — and the ghost arc (C) is covered by the fill exactly when you're over pace. Between the segment intensities, the recommendation was the A1 blend, but the user chose A2 deliberately: being over pace means the window will hit 100% at this rate, so the ≥100% red is the honest colour for that segment, just early. A2's trade-off (a 78% ring wearing the limit hue on part of its arc) was shown and accepted.
