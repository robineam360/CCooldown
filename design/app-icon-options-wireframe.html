CCBG-12 · Status Icon Swap · CCRM-42 revision

App icon options — a ring that can't be mistaken for a meter

The current icon is a 260° arc. That is a gauge at 72%, and the OS puts it in the status bar in place of the real meter. Even once CCBG-12 is fixed, an icon that reads as a filled gauge is a trap waiting for the next OS behaviour change. These options keep the ring and remove the reading.

Blast radius. This is the launcher icon — home screen, app drawer, Play listing, share card. It is not a status-bar-only change, and there is no way to make it one: the OS reaches for mipmap/ic_launcher specifically. Treat it as a revision to CCRM-42 (App Icon).

A start point, an end point, and a gap. Every convention says 72% of something. Worse, it is the same visual language as our own meter, so it reads as a real reading.

Three options

Each keeps the ring, the palette, and the stroke weight. Each is shown as the launcher icon and as the status-bar silhouette, which is where the test actually happens — the status bar renders it small and largely stripped of colour.

Remove the gap, keep everything else. No start, no end, nothing to read as progress. Smallest possible change to CCRM-42 — same geometry, same weight, one path instead of two. Risk: a plain O is generic, and the icon loses the two-tone depth the track gave it.

Closed ring plus a filled bore. Reads as a target or a record mark, never a gauge — the filled centre is the opposite of the meter's empty bore, which is what makes it distinct from our own widget faces. Keeps two tones. Risk: at 15 px the dot and ring can close up on low-density screens; the bore gap needs a floor.

option C

Four equal arcs with four equal gaps, rotationally symmetric. Symmetry is the trick — a gauge needs an origin, and this has none. Reads as a dial or a timer face, which is on-message for a cooldown app. Risk: the busiest of the three at 15 px, and the gaps can fill in on a low-density status bar.

Considered and rejected

rejected

Symmetric gap at the base

Still a gauge — a speedometer instead of a ring gauge. It moves the origin, it doesn't remove it.

rejected

Ring with a filled disc

Reads as 100% full — the same failure as today, at the far end of the scale.

rejected

Concentric double ring

Not a gauge, but the two rings merge into a blur below about 18 px. Fails the size that matters.

Worth naming: option A's weakness is real. A closed ring is exactly what our own meter looks like at 100%, so in the failure case the icon would read as "maxed out" rather than as a neutral mark. B and C do not have that collision, which is the argument for spending the extra path.

Needs deciding

2. Does the notification small icon ic_stat_bars (currently three bars, unrelated to the ring) move to match the new mark, or is the family split deliberate?
