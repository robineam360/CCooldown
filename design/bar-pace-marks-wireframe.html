CCRM-43 (Bar Pace Marks) · wireframe for approval · 2026-08-13 · rev B

Pace marks on the horizontal bars

The neutral even-pace tick and the red over-pace segment, already shipped on the rings (CCRM-39 (Ring Widget) / CCRM-40 (Mini-Rings Widget)), translated to every bar surface: the in-app usage screen, both bar widgets, and the pinned notification. Plus three Settings toggles for the red segment — one per surface. Geometry per the Mac handover (CCM-50 [Panel] / CCM-51 [Pace]).

Everything below is drawn by one routine that mirrors the proposed BarGeometry, at 2 px per dp, so the proportions are the real ones. Dead zone PACE_DEAD_ZONE = 3.0, strict. The phone / widget / notification frames are drawn in dp and shown at the same 2×, so a bar's size relative to its surface is the real one.

8 · Decisions this wireframe makes

  • D1 · Subtitles on the three toggle rows. The handover specifies one control with a footnote; three rows need to say which surface each one is. Proposed: "Bars and rings on the home screen" / "The usage screen's bars" / "The always-on notification's bars".
  • D2 · The widgets toggle covers rings too. The rings ship with the red segment today; leaving them ungoverned would mean a home screen where the bar widget obeys the setting and the ring widget doesn't.
  • D3 · The red starts exactly on the pace line, and the split is a straight edge. Robin, 2026-08-13 — this rev. Two consequences, both deliberate departures from the Mac handover: (a) no minimum width — the segment is exactly tick→fill edge, so a 3-point overshoot draws a 3-point sliver rather than being inflated to h and dragged back past the tick; (b) no capsule end at the split — the red is clipped to the fill's own shape, giving a square vertical boundary where the two colours meet and a right end that is the fill's rounded cap. The pace line is the tick's centre, so the tick straddles the split — left half over the fill, right half over the red — and the boundary lands on the mark itself at every width. Worth telling the Mac side, since CCM-50 [Panel] specifies the capsule and the h floor.
  • D4 · The collapsed notification bar stops being stretched. Confirmed this rev. Today it renders at w = h × 80 and is fitXY'd, which is harmless for a plain capsule but squeezes the tick from about 2.5 dp to 1.5 dp (both tiles in §5). It now renders at the real pixel width with no stretch, which also keeps the fill→red boundary a true vertical.
  • D5 · Bitmaps gain horizontal padding too. A tick at 0% or 100% is centred on the bar's end, so half of it falls outside the track. The bar surfaces inset by tickWidth/2 horizontally as well as 0.3h vertically, rather than clamping the tick inward and lying about its position.
  • D6 · Credits are unmarked everywhere — in-app, tall widget, notification. Carried straight from the handover.
  • D7 · The shipped rings adopt D3's straight split too. RingRenderer draws its red arc with the fill's round cap still set, so today the red bulges about 0.35 × stroke backwards past the tick. §6b shows the corrected version: the segment keeps its round tip at the fill end and is trimmed at the tick by re-drawing the fill colour over [0, tick] with a butt cap. Without this, bars and rings would disagree about where the red begins.
Rev B changes (rev A → rev B, 2026-08-13):
  1. Red starts exactly on the pace line — the min-width floor is gone (D3).
  2. The fill→red boundary is a straight vertical edge, not a capsule end; the red's far end is the fill's own cap (D3).
  3. Same rule applied to the rings, which needed a cap fix to honour it (D7).
  4. D4 resolved: the collapsed notification bar renders unstretched. D1 resolved: the three subtitles stay as shown.
