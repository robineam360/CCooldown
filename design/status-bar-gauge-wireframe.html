Twin rings in the status-bar icon

The always-on notification's small icon — the one at the top left of the status bar — showing both windows instead of one, each with a pace mark, and no numbers. The per-model third element is out of scope for this revision.

Today that icon is UsageIcon.draw: a 24 dp monochrome mask of the 5-hour window only, in one of four styles (Ring / Pie / Battery / Number), with no pace mark. The 7-day reading and its elapsed fraction are already computed at the call site (data.weekly, elapsedPercent(…, WEEKLY_MS)), so this is a drawing change, not a data one.

Three constraints the Mac's menu bar doesn't have. They decide most of this page.
  1. The icon is an alpha mask. Android tints it — white on a dark bar, black on a light one — and throws your colours away. The entire severity ladder cannot exist on this surface. Not "looks worse"; cannot exist. §3 is about what replaces it.
  2. The slot is a 24 dp square, not a wide strip. Two rings side by side fit in 11 dp each. The Mac's ring is 15 pt on a display an arm away.
  3. Fill and tick are the same white. A "neutral foreground" tick drawn on top of the fill is invisible here. The Mac's cleared gap stops being a style choice and becomes the only mechanism that works — see §4.

3 · Severity with no colour available

The bar strips colour, so 84% and 62% are the same icon unless the shape changes. Four ways to spend shape on it, at 8×. The notification body below the bar keeps its full colour ladder either way — this is only about the glyph.

Dark · each shown at 62 / 84 / 100
S0 — nothing. Fill is the only signal; 100% is a closed ring. Quietest, and consistent with the icon never having shouted. But 99 and 100 look identical.
S1 — the 12 o'clock notch. At ≥100 a cut opens at the top, so a closed ring is visibly closed rather than merely long. Recommended. Costs nothing below 100.
S2 — a hollow dot at ≥100. Reads instantly, but at 11 dp the dot eats the ring's middle and the two states share no visual family.
S3 — swap to "!!". What the Number style already does at ≥100. Loud and unmistakable, but the rings vanish exactly when you most want to see the other window.

4 · The pace mark — why it has to be a cut

On a coloured surface a tick can be drawn over the fill in a neutral colour. Here there is one ink. Drawn over the fill, a white tick on white fill is nothing at all. At 8×, over a filled stretch of ring:

Dark · 62% fill, tick at 45% — the tick falls inside the fill
Drawn on top. Invisible. Same ink as the fill it sits on. This is the Mac's overhang/house tick, ported naively.
Cut through. Works. The band is erased to transparency, so the bar shows through — legible on fill and on bare track alike. The Mac's cleared gap, doing real work.
External pip. Also works, and survives at smaller sizes — but it steals 2 dp of diameter from the ring and reads as a second object.
Coasting. Fill short of the cut. On bare track the cut is a gap in a faint circle — subtle, but it's the good news case.

5 · States

Every state is the same 24 dp square. No data draws a bare track — never a full ring, never a dashed one, never a fake 0%. Shown in L1; L0 and L2 follow the same rules.

Dark · 3×
7-day has no reading. Bare track on the right ring, keeping its slot. The notification text says "7-day no data" in words.
No reset clock. Elapsed can’t be derived, so no cut at all on that ring — never a guessed position. Fills still draw.
No data at all (first poll, or a dead token). Two bare tracks. The pinned notification is already suppressed when signed out, so there is no signed-out icon.
5-hour at 100, 7-day at 93. With S1, the 12 o'clock notch marks the closed ring. Colour is where this surface has nothing to give — the panel below carries it.
2% and 0%. The 9%-minimum fill keeps a 2% window visible; a true 0% draws nothing but track, which is correct — nothing has been spent.

7 · Settings, and what doesn't change

  • The style menu grows by one. Settings → Status-bar icon currently offers Ring · Pie · Battery · Number. Proposed: add Twin as a fifth chip, default unchanged (Ring stays the default; nobody's icon changes without them choosing it). The alternative — teaching the existing Ring chip to draw two rings — silently changes an icon people have already picked.
  • The pace cut rides along with every style, or only with Twin? Proposed: the cut is added to Ring and Twin (they have a band to cut), and Pie / Battery / Number are left alone — there is nowhere sensible to put it on a pie slice or two bold digits.
  • "Show red past the pace mark" (paceOverOnNotification) governs the notification's colour gauge and is untouched. It cannot reach the glyph.
  • Accessibility is unchanged — the icon has never been the accessible surface; the notification's title, text and panel carry every number, and already read both windows.
  • Which account is settled: the icon follows cache.pinnedProfile(), exactly as the notification does.
  • Nothing about the notification body changes — its colour gauge, bars and pace marks (CCRM-43 (Bar Pace Marks)) stay as shipped.
Decisions — approved 2026-08-21, built the same day:
  1. Layout: L2 concentric, with both rings pushed to the maximum the 24 dp square allows — 7-day outer at 23 dp, 5-hour inner at 14.5 dp, both at a 2.6 dp stroke — per "make the rings as big as possible so the pace mark is clear on both windows".
  2. Severity: S1 — a notch opens at 12 o'clock at ≥100, so a closed ring reads as closed.
  3. Pace mark: the cut, erased through the band (PorterDuff.CLEAR), 0.6 × stroke wide.
  4. Style menu: a fifth chip, Twin. Ring stays the default; nobody's icon changes without them choosing it.
  5. Quick Settings tile follows the same style — one setting, one look.
  6. The cut is also added to the existing Ring style (it has a band to cut); Pie / Battery / Number are untouched.
