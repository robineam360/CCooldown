CCBG-12 · Status Icon Swap

Notification grouping — keeping the live meter in the status bar

A second notification from the app makes Android swap our live meter for the launcher icon, which is a ring frozen at 72%. Fixing it means owning the notification group, and that changes what the shade looks like. This sketch is about that change.

The icon at the heart of it

The launcher ring is a fixed 260° arc. It is not a meter, but at 24 dp it reads exactly like one — which is why the swap misinforms rather than merely looking wrong.

Proposal — the pinned notification becomes the group summary

One group, owned by us, with the pinned notification as its summary. That suppresses the system's auto-summary and hands the status-bar slot back to our bitmap. Measured on the Fold 7: the meter survives with three alerts posted.

The alternative, if the collapse is unacceptable

Don't group. Instead keep the app to one notification most of the time, so the swap has no chance to happen: give alerts setTimeoutAfter so they clear themselves, and fold the long-lived ones (sign-in expiry) into the pinned notification's expanded panel rather than posting them separately.
