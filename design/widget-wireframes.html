Three home-screen widget faces

Android ports of the Mac desktop widget family (CCRM-18 [Desktop]) with pace marks on every ring (CCM-49 [Desktop]), per ANDROID-WIDGET-HANDOVER.md. These ship as three new widget providers alongside the existing two — picker names Ring, Mini-rings, Pace chart. Faces are drawn at 1 px = 1 dp; every board is shown in the widget's light and dark themes. Fixture numbers are the shared ones from the handover §6 so these tiles can sit next to the Mac renders.

  • Pace marks, all rings: a neutral tick at the even-pace point (foreground at 48% light / 60% dark, width max(2, 0.31·stroke), overhanging 0.3·stroke past both edges), plus a full-red segment from tick to fill tip only when percent > elapsed + 3.0 — the same dead-zoned comparison the chart wash and pace sentence use. Draw order: track · fill · red segment · tick. No percent or no reset clock → no tick at all.
  • Severity ladder (independent of pace): accent ≤80 · yellow >80 · orange >90 · red ≥100. Percent always truncates.
  • Reset shown both ways on every face; countdowns are two-unit compact and soften to "soon" inside five minutes.
  • Refresh ↻ asks the app to poll (180-second floor applies); widgets never touch the network.
  • Update cadence: redraw after every poll + a 15-minute redraw-only tick + a one-shot redraw at the reset moment when it's <20 min away. No per-second/minute alarms — "soon" is what makes that honest.
  • Mac's needsAppUpdate state is omitted: Glance widgets render in the app process from the same APK, so snapshot-vs-binary skew cannot happen on Android. Recorded in code.

1 · Ring widget — small (2×2)

One profile + one window, chosen in the config activity. The ring is the hero; everything reads inside it. No window title on this face. ↻ overlays the top-right corner.

  • Cell 2×2, min 110×110dp, resizable. Ring stroke 8dp; hero percent 25sp medium, tabular digits; countdown 12sp secondary; exact clock 10sp tertiary.
  • Profile caps-label (8.5sp) shows only when both profiles are signed in — tiles 1 vs 2 below.
  • Stale / fetch error keep the full-size ring; one amber pill overlays its bottom edge (rev 2, your call), one line, truncating.
  • noData: bare track, em dash, "Waiting for first fetch". notSignedIn: text-only face.

2 · Mini-rings widget — medium (4×2)

Every window of one profile, battery-style. Ignores the configured window; binds the configured profile. Payload order session · weekly · model caps, capped at four columns so the two fixed windows always survive. Each ring carries its own pace tick — same glance, different verdicts, which is the whole feature.

  • Cell 4×2, min 250×110dp. Mini-rings 58dp, stroke 5.5dp; percent inside 13sp; title 10.5sp; compact countdown 9.5sp. No prose reset line — density is the point.
  • Header: profile label · "Updated Xm ago" + ↻. Stale/fetchError turn the updated text into the amber pill.
  • Session tick vs the 5h clock; weekly and every model cap tick vs the 7d clock.

3 · Pace widget — large (4×3)

One window, the pace story: the app's CCRM-12/CCRM-20 chart plus a verdict sentence. The 5h/7d toggle works on the face itself, beats the configured window on this face only, and persists (works with the app swiped away).

  • Cell 4×3, min 250×180dp. Percent 34sp; countdown + exact reset stacked right; footer "Updated Xm ago" + ↻.
  • Chart: x = window lifetime 0→1, y fixed 0–100 (never autoscaled); even-pace diagonal always; guides 80/90/100 in ladder colours; one dot per real sample (polling gaps show); dashed projection with hollow labelled endpoint; "now" hairline; red wash over the above-diagonal region at 7% light / 10% dark, drawn only when at-or-above pace (dead-zoned).
  • Pace sentence goes amber when the verdict is above. A refused projection prints why — never a silently missing chart.
