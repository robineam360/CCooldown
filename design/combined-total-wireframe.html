CCRM-31 · Combined Total · rev B · spent-only · needs approval

One reading across every account

Nothing in the app answers how much have I spent in total. This proposes the smallest honest answer: a one-line total on the credits card that also opens a Total spend screen, where a share donut — one slice per account, descending, with the 2.5% floor — says who spent it. Every figure on the screen is a real spend figure. Every number below is a worked example, not a placeholder.

rev B — the balance problem is designed out, not argued past. Rev A kept a combined denominator: 53% of $190.00 in the ring centre and $90.22 left in the footer. Both leaned on the monthly cap standing in for a credit balance the API does not give us — spend.balance is null in every payload, and the endpoint that carries it rejects our bearer (CCBG-6 (Credits Denominator), Won't fix). So both lines are gone.
  • Spent comes from spend.used.amount_minor — present, real, not in doubt. This is the question CCRM-31 was filed to answer, and it needs nothing we don't have.
  • Left would need spend.balance. Not available, so not claimed.
  • The share ring is unaffected — it never needed a denominator, which is why it is a share rather than a gauge.
Two rev-A states — "some accounts uncapped" and "at the combined cap" — stop existing, because nothing measures against a cap any more. Decided 2026-08-27.

1 · Where it lives

Three homes were on the table in the roadmap. Two are wrong for the same reason: the reading is account-independent, and both the main pager and the History screen are per-account surfaces with a profile tab strip.

OptionVerdictWhy
A third pager pageNo Reopens the CCRM-20 (Wide Chart) question of what the pager is for, and the roadmap already leaned against it. Worse: an "All" tab in the strip would be a 4th tab at three accounts, tripping FIXED_TAB_LIMIT and turning a fixed strip scrollable for everyone.
On the History screenNo History is windows over time and carries its own per-profile strip. Money is not history, and it would inherit a tab strip it has no use for.
A card at the top of each profileNo Pushes the 5-hour window — the question you actually open the app with — below the fold, to answer a rarer and more reflective question.
An entry row on the existing credits card → its own screen Proposed The one-line answer appears exactly where the question arises, costs 44 dp inside a card that already exists, no app-bar slot, and no strip. The ring gets a full screen instead of fighting a card for room. It is unreachable when no account has credits — which is correct: there is nothing to total.

· Ring 176 dp across, 22 dp stroke, extent track at 10% ink. Starts at 12 o'clock, clockwise, descending by spend — the biggest payer owns the first arc, so the answer to "who spent it" is where the eye already is.

· 3 dp gap between slices, taken off the end of each arc.

· Centre: headlineMedium total, bodySmall "across N accounts". No percentage — there is no denominator, and the ring is already a share.

· Legend rows 36 dp: 12 dp swatch, label, money, share right at 60% alpha. Share is rounded and may not sum to 100%; the money is the truth and is exact.

· Product's real share is 1.3%. The floor lifts it to 2.5% so it stays a visible arc rather than a hairline — MINIMUM_SPEND_SLICE_SHARE earning its keep, which the roadmap noted it barely did at two accounts. The legend still prints 1%: the floor is a drawing rule, never a number.

· No severity anywhere. An account at its own cap still draws in its accent tint. The ring's one channel is share; the warning ladder lives on the per-account card, which is the only place a cap exists.

4 · Slice colour

No per-account colour exists anywhere in the app, and inventing four hues would break the single-accent grammar CCRM-3 (Unified Theming) enforces. Proposal: the theme accent at four descending strengths, assigned by descending spend — 100%, 62%, 34%, 20% alpha — with a fifth-and-beyond slice at 20% and distinguished only by the legend.

The known weakness. At four slices the 34% and 20% tints are close, and on the Amber theme they are close and pale — the same thinness that CCBG-15 (Amber Ladder Blindness) records. The mitigation is that the ring is a share, not a value to read off: the legend carries every number, and the swatch only has to tie a row to an arc. Q4 offers the alternative.

The happy path is the least interesting part. These are the cases the surface has to survive — and per the working agreement, they are the reason the wireframe exists at all. Rev B has six, down from eight: some accounts uncapped and at the combined cap cannot occur once nothing is measured against a cap.

FileHolds

TotalSpendScreen.ktCompose, both width classes.
MainActivity.ktScreen.TOTAL, the entry row on the credits card, the app-bar title.
The roadmap's extraction note is stale. CCRM-31 says it "shares the ring drawing with CCRM-3 phase 3 and CCRM-13 — whichever lands first owns the Canvas-to-bitmap extraction". CCRM-39 (Ring Widget) already landed that: ui/RingRenderer.kt is the shared bitmap ring. But a multi-slice donut is different geometry, not a parameter of a single-value ring, and this one is drawn in Compose on a live screen rather than rasterised for Glance. So CCRM-31 reuses RingRenderer's stroke and gap conventions and adds its own geometry; it does not inherit or owe the extraction. The roadmap line needs correcting when this is built.

Not in scope, deliberately: no widget face, no notification line, no tile — a reflective monthly reading has no business on a glanceable surface, and CCRM-24 (Share Card) is where an exportable version would belong if ever. Alerts are untouched.

8 · Questions

Q1 — answered 2026-08-27

Does the missing credit balance block this? No — it removes half the feature instead. spend.used is available and real; spend.balance is not. So the combined percentage and the combined "left" line are gone, and what remains needs nothing the API doesn't give us. Rev B above.

Q1b — answered 2026-08-27

Should the combined monthly cap appear anywhere? Not at all — spent-only. Per-account caps stay on each profile's credits card, where they are scoped to the account that set them and cannot be misread as a combined ceiling.

Q2 — open

Entry row on the credits card, or a third app-bar icon? The row costs no icon slot and puts the answer where the question is, but it is only discoverable by someone who has credits and scrolls to the card. A third icon next to History and Settings is discoverable but permanent, and meaningless for anyone with no credit budget. Recommend: the row.

Q3 — open

Does an account with credits switched off count into the total? Precedent says no — the toggle already suppresses the widget bar, so it reads as "not this account's money, anywhere" — and the count line would say "2 of 3". The counter-argument is that a thing called total should be total. Recommend: exclude, and say so in the count.

Q4 — open

Accent tints for slices, or four distinct hues? Tints keep one accent and work on all twelve themes; four hues are unambiguous at a glance but introduce a second colour system and will collide with some accents. Recommend: tints — the legend carries every number, so the swatch only has to tie a row to an arc.

Q5 — open

Descending by spend, or fixed by account slot? Descending puts the biggest payer at 12 o'clock and answers the question fastest; fixed by slot means a slice keeps its colour and position between visits, so a change in the ring means a change in spending rather than a reshuffle. Recommend: descending, since this is a screen you visit occasionally, not one you watch.
Nothing gets built until Q2–Q5 are answered. Per working agreement 2, silence is not approval — and if an answer is "change X", the revised wireframe comes before any code.
