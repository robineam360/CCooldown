What it does

📊Both limit windows, live5-hour session bar + 7-day bars (all models and per-model caps like Fable), with exact reset day & time for each.
📅Usage historyA scrollable bar for every 5-hour session, week by week — see how many you ran and which ones hit 100%.
📌Pinned notificationAn optional always-on, silent notification with a status-bar gauge that fills as you use your 5-hour window.
📈Burn-rate forecastA sparkline of your usage curve and a plain-words projection: "At this pace: 100% at Thu 2:40 PM — 1h 20m before the reset."
🔔Granular alertsPer-window threshold chips, reset pings set off / if-busy / always, and a per-profile mute — quiet when you want, loud when it counts.
⏱️The pacing bar"Days elapsed" sits under your weekly bars — usage behind it means headroom, ahead of it means slow down.
👥Two accounts, editablePersonal (Pro/Max) and Work (Team) as fully independent profiles — separate tabs, widgets, tiles and alerts; rename them freely.
🔐Sign in on the phoneThe same secure OAuth flow Claude Code uses, right from the phone — no computer, no copied tokens, self-renews monthly.
💾Private & backed upNo servers or telemetry; the token is Keystore-encrypted. History & settings now survive a reinstall (token excluded).

How it was made

CCooldown exists because of one recurring pain: deep in a Claude chat or a Cowork run, the dreaded "approaching your limit" — with no idea how close, or when the window resets. (Claude Code can tell you; the chat and Cowork apps can't.) The fix: put the answer where your eyes already are — the home screen.

Claude Fable 5 built it — in a weekend. After a week of ideas and throwaway prototypes, one weekend turned it real: pushed to git, signed, running on an actual phone. Fable wrote virtually everything — the app, the Glance widgets, the alert logic, even the reverse-engineering of the undocumented usage API.

Then it hit the one wall Fable wouldn't cross: the OAuth sign-in handshake, which Fable declined on cybersecurity grounds. Claude Opus 4.8 stepped in — built the native sign-in the app now uses, and wrote the docs Fable left undone. If you pay for Claude, you already have the engineer… or two.

Want it on your phone?

It's free & open-source. Scan the code (or visit github.com/robineam360/CCooldown), grab the latest APK from the Releases page, and follow the detailed user guide — install → sign-in → widgets in ~3 minutes. Android 12+ today; iOS is on the roadmap.

And if the "built by Claude" part is the bit that got you curious — even better. Bring an idea of your own; it probably takes fewer evenings than you think.
